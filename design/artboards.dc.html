summary edit
Stem bark of Khaya senegalensis is among the most widely traded medicinal barks of the West African savanna, sold in dried plates and taken as a bitter decoction. Recorded use clusters around malarial fever, dysentery and inflammatory joint pain, with veterinary use for trypanosomiasis in cattle documented across the Sahel. Limonoid bitters are the presumed active class; one controlled trial in uncomplicated malaria exists, and everything below that line rests on uncontrolled or traditional record.

preparation
